Read alongside every package for a complete, no-surprise scope.

  • Compound wall

    Chargeable if total construction area is under 4,000 sq.ft, or for extra height.

  • Plinth & sump

    Floor height up to 18″ from road level is included. Outside ramping and extra sump depth are charged separately.

  • Soil bearing capacity

    Standard design is based on SBC ≥ 180 kN/m². Additional structural design applies if SBC is below 180.

  • Bathroom ratio

    1 bathroom per 500 sq.ft is included; additional bathrooms are charged on actual material & labor.

  • Rainwater harvesting

    Available as an optional provision at ₹30,000 extra where not already included by default in the package.

  • Interiors

    Modular kitchens, wardrobes and false ceilings fall under specialized interior contracts.

  • Elevation extras

    Specialized external wall cladding, dynamic facade lighting and landscaping are charged separately.

  • Electrical fittings

    End-user fittings — bulbs, tubes, fans, decorative lights — are supplied by the client.

  • Logistics

    Material double-shifting charges apply where site storage or access is constrained.
